Devil's Hole to Hawksbill Mountain: via Linville Gorge, NC

Devil's Hole Loop Trail is a 5.3 mile trail that combines the Devil's Hole, Linville Gorge, and Spencer's Ridge Trails to reach Hawksbill Mountain

This hike offers awesome views of the gorge and plenty of cool things to see on the way, including rare species and some good swimming spots. Take a good look around and you'll definitely spot an American Chestnut or Carolina Hemlock on the way!
